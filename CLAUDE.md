## Files

Read on demand. Do not load unless the work touches them.

- `project/data-contract.md` — shape of every catalogue record
- `project/api-and-schema.md` — auth, collections, rules, functions
- `project/catalogue.md` — generated coverage counts
- `project/pending.md` — planned work and open decisions

Each carries its own contract in a header block. Read the header before writing
to the file.
